About the numbers

What "water/CO2 saved" actually means

These are avoided-impact estimates, not a measurement of your specific t-shirt or jeans. They represent the gap between two scenarios: a brand-new replacement garment being manufactured from raw fiber (growing cotton, spinning, weaving, dyeing, shipping) versus your old garment being reused or recycled instead, which skips most of that. Different studies land on different per-kg figures because life-cycle assessments vary by fiber and methodology. That's normal, which is why we name one specific source rather than presenting a number as universal fact.
